# Student Centre

> [!note]
> Sample page showing the Place layout — hours and prices below are
> illustrative. Confirm before you travel.

The Student Centre is the main food-and-services building on the Potchefstroom
campus — the default meeting point ("see you at the Centre at one"). It houses
takeaway outlets, seating areas, ATMs, and a few student services counters.

*Last checked: October 2026 (sample — replace with the real month).*

## Getting there

On foot from the main gate: straight up the central walkway, big double-volume
building on your right — you can smell it before you see it at lunchtime.

### Parking

The lot in front fills by **08:15** on class days. After that, use the overflow
lot behind the sports fields and walk (about 7 minutes).

## Food and prices

Prices change often — figures below are **as of October 2026 (sample)**:

| Outlet | Typical meal | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| Main takeaway counter | Burger + chips | R65–R90 |
| Coffee kiosk | Regular coffee | R25–R35 |
| Vetkoek stand (Fridays) | Vetkoek + mince | ~R40 |

> [!warning]
> Card machines go down during load shedding more often than you'd think. Keep
> a R100 note on you on Centre days.

## Services inside

- ATMs (two banks) in the entrance foyer
- Printing kiosk — not the cheapest on campus, but open latest
- Student Representative Council office hours on Wednesdays

### Hours

Term-time hours below were last checked October 2026 (sample — confirm,
especially around holidays):

- Main hall: Mon–Fri 07:30–17:00
- Coffee kiosk: Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00, Sat 09:00–13:00

## Best and worst times

- **Best:** before 08:00 (quiet, fresh stock) or after 14:30 (queues gone).
- **Worst:** 12:00–13:30 — the queue for the main counter reaches the doors.
