# Exams, Tests & Deadlines

Assessment dates at NWU are announced through eFundi — in the module's
**Calendar**, **Assignments**, and **Announcements**. The problem isn't finding
them, it's *noticing* when one changes.

## How dates move

Lecturers change dates constantly: a test moves a week, a practical gets a
second slot, a deadline shifts after a request. The change usually appears in:

1. **Announcements** (most common — "the test has moved to…")
2. The **Calendar** tool in the module site
3. Sometimes **nowhere official** — ask in class if something feels wrong

> [!warning]
> Do not rely on memories of first-week dates. A module's timetable in week 1 is
> a plan; the calendar in week 8 is the truth. Re-check your sources before
> every test week.

## Let the robot watch it for you

The [eFundiHax](#/modules) sync keeps every module's calendar up to date and can
push events into a Google Calendar with reminders at:

- **1 day before**
- **1 hour before**
- **at the start** of the event

Tests, quizzes, practicals and exams get reminders. Consultations never do
(they're optional, and a reminder for an optional session is just noise).

To enable: install eFundiHax (see [First Weeks](#/getting-started)), enable
cookie lending, and keep at least one eFundi tab open occasionally so the sync
lanes run. Then subscribe to the module's calendar once and forget it.

## Exam-time basics

- **Exam timetable** — published by the university each semester; it is also
  announced via student email. Venue and seat number are on it; check both the
  evening before.
- **Student card + ID** — bring both to every exam. No card, no entry, no
  exceptions on the day.
- **Aegrotat / deferred exams** — if illness or tragedy hits an exam, talk to
  your faculty office *immediately* — there are processes, but they require
  documentation and time, not a same-day email.
