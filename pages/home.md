# NWU Wiki

**The student-built knowledge base for North-West University.**

eFundi holds your module content. This wiki holds everything *around* it: how
bursaries actually work, what happens in your first weeks, which deadlines
matter, and the small things that take a semester to figure out on your own.

Every page here was started by a student and can be fixed by any student —
including you, from your phone, in about two minutes. See
[How to Contribute](#/contributing).

> [!tip]
> You do not need an account, git, or coding skills to help. The
> **Edit page** button in the top-right opens GitHub's web editor with the raw
> page — fix a typo, save, done.

## What's inside

| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **First Weeks Guide** | eFundi login, installing eFundiHax, wifi, student card, the logistics nobody explains |
| **Modules & Synced Content** | Live list of every module synced by eFundiHax — notes, calendars, announcements |
| **Exams, Tests & Deadlines** | How dates flow from eFundi into your calendar automatically |
| **Bursaries Guide + Directory** | How SA bursaries work, plus the big list of who funds what |
| **Campus Life** | Residences, venues, parking, printing — stubs waiting for your knowledge |

## The two halves

This wiki is the other half of **eFundiHax**:

- **eFundiHax** (efundihax.github.io) syncs eFundi — module content, calendars,
  announcements — into one shared place for everyone.
- **NWU Wiki** (this site) is the human half: judgement, experience, and the
  institutional knowledge that no scraper can capture.

## Ground rules

- Write for a first-year who just arrived from a village school. Spelling things
  out is a feature, not condescension.
- Say *what you know*, not what you assume. If you're unsure, say so in the text.
- Never paste private info — other people's student numbers, marks, or messages.
- This is community content, not official NWU communication. For anything
  official, go through the university's own channels.
