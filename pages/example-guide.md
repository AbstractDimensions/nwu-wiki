# First Weeks Guide (template)

> [!note]
> This is an uneditable example page showing the template structure for Guide pages.

Everything you need to get from "I got accepted" to "I can actually find my notes" — written for the student who has never used an LMS before.

## What the module covers

- **eFundi** — your university's learning site. Log in with your NWU username/password. Every module appears as a **worksite** tab.
- **eFundiHax** — free userscript that syncs your modules' announcements, calendars, resources into one shared place. [Install guide](#/getting-started).
- **WiFi** — NWU-WiFi (eduroam) + NSFAS fibre in residences. Save mobile data.
- **Student card** — collect at Student Centre Week 1. Bring ID + proof of registration.
- **Student email** — `@nwu.ac.za` is official channel. Forward to the address you actually read.

## What to do in week 1

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Collect student card, activate WiFi, find lecture venues |
| Tue | Install eFundiHax userscript, enable cookie lending |
| Wed | Walk campus map (print from sidebar), find lecture venues |
| Thu | Check module sites on eFundi, download study guides |
| Fri | Find Student Centre, clinic, library, printing kiosk |

## Things that will save you a week

- **Check Announcements weekly.** Venue changes, test moves, cancellations are announced *there*, not by email.
- **Deadlines are local time.** If a due date says 23:55, it's 23:55 SAST — submit before midnight, not "during" it.
- **Download, don't stream.** If your data is limited, the eFundiHax hub lets you open synced module pages without opening eFundi itself.
- **Save the IT helpdesk number** in your phone during week 1, not the night before a submission deadline.
