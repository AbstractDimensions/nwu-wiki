## Overview

Villagers is a mixed-gender university residence on the Potchefstroom campus. Known for its strong community spirit and academic focus.

## Rooms & facilities

- 320 single rooms, 40 double rooms — all with study desk & bed
- 3 communal kitchens, 2 laundry rooms, study hall, braai area
- Free WiFi (NSFAS fibre), laundry card system

## Rules & culture

- Quiet hours 22:00–07:00 (strictly enforced)
- No alcohol in common areas
- Sign-in/out after 23:00
- House committee runs events: movie nights, braais, study groups

## How to apply

- NSFAS portal by **30 September** (year before)
- R1 200 deposit (refundable)
- Preference given to 1st-years and NSFAS students

## Tips

- Room 200s = quiet wing
- Laundry best on Tue mornings (machines free)
- SRC rep = first point of contact for issues
