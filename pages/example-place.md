# Student Centre (template)

> [!note]
> This is an uneditable example page showing the template structure for Places pages.

## Getting there

On foot from the main gate: straight up the central walkway, big double-volume building on your right — you can smell it before you see it at lunchtime.

### Parking

The lot in front fills by **08:15** on class days. After that, use the overflow lot behind the sports fields and walk (about 7 minutes).

## Food and prices

Prices change often — figures below are **as of 2026**:

| Outlet | Typical meal | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| Main takeaway counter | Burger + chips | R65–R90 |
| Coffee kiosk | Regular coffee | R25–R35 |
| Vetkoek stand (Fridays) | Vetkoek + mince | ~R40 |

> [!warning]
> Card machines go down during load shedding more often than you'd think. Keep a R100 note on you on Centre days.

## Services inside

- ATMs (two banks) in the entrance foyer
- Printing kiosk — not the cheapest on campus, but open latest
- Student Representative Council office hours on Wednesdays

## Best and worst times

- **Best:** before 08:00 (quiet, fresh stock) or after 14:30 (queues gone).
- **Worst:** 12:00–13:30 — the queue for the main counter reaches the doors.
