# Modules & Synced Content

The list below is **live** — it's pulled straight from the eFundiHax backend
every time this page loads, so it shows exactly what the community has synced
right now, not a stale copy in a git commit.

<div id="efh-live-modules"></div>

## What "synced" means

For every module in the list, eFundiHax keeps a page at the
[eFundiHax hub](https://efundihax.github.io) containing:

- **Announcements** — everything lecturers posted
- **Calendar events** — tests, practicals, assignment due dates
- **Resources & lesson content** — text and images extracted from PDFs,
  slides and documents
- **Master doc** — one Google Doc per module you can hand straight to AI study
  tools (NotebookLM etc.) or read on your phone

## Missing module?

If your module isn't in the list:

1. Install the eFundiHax userscript (see [First Weeks Guide](#/getting-started)).
2. Open the module in eFundi while the script is running.
3. Refresh this page after the sync indicators show "Up to date" — it appears
   automatically.

> [!tip]
> Syncing is **communal**: once one student with the script opens a module,
> every student can see that module's synced info — including students in the
> same module who haven't installed anything.

## Notes per module?

This is where community knowledge compounds: if there's a known trap in a
module ("practicals must be booked by week 3", "the test is open-book"), that
belongs on the module's page. Ask in the eFundiHax hub's feedback link, or open
a wiki page and link it from here.
