# Rag Week (template)

> [!note]
> This is an uneditable example page showing the template structure for Traditions & Events pages.

## What happens

Week-long charity drive + festivities; residences compete in skits, floats, fundraising; ends with Rag Ball.

## When & where

- **When:** First week of March (Mon–Fri)
- **Campus:** Potchefstroom
- **Main venue:** Main quad & amphitheatre

## History

Started 1962 as "Rag Procession" for charity; grew to week-long; 2023 raised R2.3M for 12 charities.

## Tips for first-timers

- Wear res colours Mon–Thu
- Bring cash for stalls
- Rag Ball tickets sell out Day 1
- Hydrate (March heat!)
