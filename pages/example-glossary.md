# Wildcat (template)

> This is an uneditable example page showing the template structure for Glossary pages.

The unofficial energy drink of late-night study sessions — a sweet, high-caffeine canned drink sold at tuck shops and vending machines around campus. Mention "pulling a Wildcat" and every student knows you mean an all-nighter.

## Variants

- **Original** — the classic green can
- **Sugar-free** — black can, tastes like compromise *(unconfirmed)*

### Price

Usually R20–R30 per can at campus tuck shops *(unconfirmed — please check)*.

## Health note

> [!warning]
> Two cans is already over most daily caffeine guidance. The all-nighter it buys you is paid back with interest the next day. Water between cans is the student body's collective hard-won advice.
