# Category Templates

Every category has a default shape: an infobox plus a fixed set of sections.
New pages should start from their category's template so readers always know
where to look. This page IS the contract — refine it and the wiki follows.

> [!note]
> Infobox data lives in `pages.json` (photo + facts); sections live in the
> page's `.md` file. Required infobox fields are enforced on save.

## Lecturers

Infobox: photo, faculty, campus, office, email (official only), classes.

- **Classes** — modules taught, with codes and semesters
- **Consultation** — hours, booking arrangement, what to bring
- **Assessment style** — formats, weights if published, exam rules
- **Teaching style** — describe, don't rate; link the study guide

## Places

Infobox: photo, campus, building, hours.

- **Getting there** — walking route from a known landmark; parking subsection
- **Hours** — term-time hours with last-checked month
- **Services & prices** — what's inside, prices with as-of month
- **Tips** — best/worst times, load-shedding quirks, cash vs card

## Residences

Infobox: photo, campus, type (residence / day residence / private accredited),
established.

- **Overview** — who lives here, vibe in one paragraph
- **Rooms & facilities** — room types, kitchen, laundry, wifi, parking
- **Rules & culture** — house rules that actually get enforced, traditions
- **How to apply** — application route and deadlines with year
- **Tips** — what seniors wish they'd known in week 1

## Modules

Infobox: code, faculty, credits, synced-notes link (eFundiHax hub).

- **What the module covers** — topics; kit/materials subsection where relevant
- **Assessment breakdown** — weights from the study guide ONLY, flagged illustrative otherwise
- **Lecturers** — interlinked lecturer pages
- **How to pass** — study tactics that worked
- **Known traps** — recurring exam/test pitfalls
- **Links** — synced hub page, eFundi portal
- **Resources** — master doc, schedule sheet, study guide location

## University Programs

Infobox: code, faculty, duration, qualification type.

- **Overview** — what the degree is and who it's for
- **Curriculum structure** — years, majors, streams
- **Admission requirements** — APS and subjects with year
- **Career paths** — where graduates go
- **Links** — faculty handbook, NWU programme page

## Societies

Infobox: photo, campus, type, meeting time.

- **About** — what the society does
- **How to join** — sign-up route, fees, requirements
- **Meetings & activities** — when, where, what a term looks like
- **Membership** — costs, commitment level

## Traditions & Events

Infobox: photo, when, campus.

- **What happens** — the event in one paragraph
- **When & where** — dates/period, venues
- **History** — origin and how it changed
- **Tips for first-timers** — what to bring, expect, avoid

## Glossary

No infobox required (optional image). Short by design.

- **Meaning** — the definition first, one paragraph
- **Details** — variants, prices, related facts (each marked confirmed/unconfirmed)
- **See also** — interlinked pages

## Guides

Free-form. How-tos follow no template, but every Guide should state WHO it is
for in the first paragraph and carry dates with years, never "recently".
