# Campus Life

This page is a **stub on purpose**. Campus knowledge is exactly what a wiki is
for: the parking spot that fills by 08:15, the printing shop that's cheapest,
which residence cafeteria opens late during exams. Nobody official will ever
write that down. You will.

## The shape of NWU

North-West University has **three campuses** — Potchefstroom, Mahikeng and
Vanderbijlpark — plus sites like the Mafikeng and Vaal Triangle operations.
Most undergraduate teaching happens on the three main campuses, and your
**timetable tells you which buildings and venues** you'll use. Walking your
first-week timetable *before* classes start is the single best hour you'll
spend.

## Worth documenting here

Pick a section you know and fill it in:

- **Residences** — which ones exist, what to bring, how the sub-min / catering
  works, what the rules actually are in practice
- **Printing & copying** — cheapest per page on/near campus, which library
  printers charge what
- **Parking & transport** — permit rules, the lots that fill first, shuttle or
  taxi routes students actually use
- **Food** — cheap eats near each campus, student meal options
- **Sport & societies** — how to join, what trials cost
- **Health & safety** — campus clinic hours, campus security numbers
- **Internet** — wifi network names, how to get help, eduroam if available

> [!tip]

## Ground rules for this page

- No other people's private details — residences, plate numbers, room numbers.
- Prices change; add "as of 2026" to anything with a number in it.
- If it's only true for one campus, say which.
