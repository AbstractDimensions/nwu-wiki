# First Weeks Guide

Everything you need to get from "I got accepted" to "I can actually find my
notes" — written for the student who has never used an LMS before.

## 1. eFundi — your university's learning site

eFundi is NWU's learning management system (it runs on a platform called
Sakai). Every module you're registered for appears there as a **worksite**.

1. Go to **efundi.nwu.ac.za**
2. Log in with your NWU username and password (the same ones you use for
   student email and the self-service portal)
3. You'll land on **My Workspace** — your home base. The **tab bar** at the top
   lists your module worksites. If a module isn't there yet, registration may
   still be processing; check again in a day or two.

Inside any module site, the left-hand menu is where the content lives:
**Announcements**, **Resources**, **Lessons** (sometimes called "Module
Content"), **Assignments**, **Tests & Quizzes**, **Calendar**, and
**Gradebook**.

> [!warning]
> If eFundi asks you to accept an honour pledge before opening an assignment or
> test, read it — it is a real academic-integrity agreement. Never let someone
> else complete a test on your account, even a friend, even a "study group".

## 2. eFundiHax — never miss module content again

**eFundiHax** is a free userscript (a small browser add-on) built by NWU
students. It syncs your modules' announcements, calendars, resources and lesson
content into one shared place that everyone with the script can browse — and
pushes your deadlines into a Google Calendar with reminders.

To install:

1. Install the **Tampermonkey** browser extension (on Chrome/Edge: from the
   Chrome Web Store; on Android you can use a Tampermonkey-supporting browser).
2. Open the install page: **efundihax.github.io** → "Install eFundiHax" and
   click the install button. Accept the prompts.
3. Log into eFundi and open one of your modules. You'll see small **sync
   indicators** appear — when they show "Up to date", that module's content is
   synced.
4. To get your dates into a real calendar, enable **cookie lending** from the
   Tampermonkey menu ("eFundiHax: Enable cookie lending"). This lets the
   backend keep fetching schedule updates while you're offline. You can revoke
   it at any time (menu → revoke, or just change your eFundi password).

> [!note]
> Cookie lending is optional and consent-based. The script only sends your
> session cookie to the eFundiHax backend — never your password — and it is
> used only to read module pages on your behalf.

## 3. Things that will save you a week

- **Check Announcements weekly.** Venue changes, test moves and cancellations
  are announced *there*, not by email.
- **Deadlines are local time.** If a due date says 23:55, it's 23:55 SAST —
  submit before midnight, not "during" it.
- **Download, don't stream.** If your data is limited, the eFundiHax hub lets
  you open synced module pages without opening eFundi itself.
- **Save the IT helpdesk number** in your phone during week 1, not the night
  before a submission deadline.

## 4. Student email

Your `@nwu.ac.za` / student email is the university's official channel. Check
it or forward it to the address you actually read — official notices
(exam timetables, fee statements, bursary letters) go there.
